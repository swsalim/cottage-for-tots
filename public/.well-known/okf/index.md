# CottageForTots

CottageForTots (cottagefortots.com) is an online-only parenting blog about how toys, books and play support early childhood development, for parents and caregivers of children from birth to about age six. Articles cover motor skills, pretend play, social-emotional learning, screen time, reading aloud and toy safety and care. The site also links to its Singapore store on Shopee (https://shopee.sg/cottagefortots); the blog is informational and not a product catalogue.
