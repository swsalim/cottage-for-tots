# Educational toys and play in early childhood development

CottageForTots writes about play-based learning: a toy's value comes from the actions it invites (grasping, threading, building, pretending, taking turns), not from an "educational" label. Coverage includes fine motor and gross motor skills, social-emotional learning, cooperative play, self-help skills for autistic children, screen-time balance, reading aloud, music toys, and safe toys for infants.
